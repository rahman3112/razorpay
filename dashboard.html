Real bugs we found and fixed today

Got slow at real volume
The matching logic compared every record against every other record — fine at 60 transactions, far too slow past 5,000. Rewrote it to look records up directly instead.
2,616 records/sec
252,000+ /sec
The AI wasn't really detecting duplicates
It was quietly reading a leftover marker in our own test data, not genuine signal. We removed the marker, watched detection disappear, and replaced it with a plain rule instead.
looked accurate
100% verified
A too-generous rule was hiding real problems
Our "this is just a fee" rule was wide enough to also wave through genuine payment discrepancies as if they were fine. Tightened it to match what a real fee actually looks like.

Why our headline number is lower than it used to be
Earlier today this showed 87%. That number was wrong — it was counting some real payment problems as clean matches. 80% is the honest number, double-checked against a known-correct answer key. We'd rather show you a correct number that's lower than a wrong one that looks better.
